About

I'm a Strategy and Analytics professional based in Ireland, with a background that reaches across data analytics, operations, risk, and financial services . I work with SQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI to transform complex data into insights that people can actually act on.

My path has taken me from transform raw data into structured insights to support decisions at PwC to customer data analytics at Eaton and now in Ireland. Along the way I've built KPI frameworks, supported forecasting and planning, analyzed seasonality cycles and presented data-driven recommendations directly to managers and CEOs.

I'm currently completing a Master's in Data Analytics in Technological University of the Shannon and open to Data Analyst roles where a genuine curiosity about the data is valued. If that sounds like your team, let's talk.
